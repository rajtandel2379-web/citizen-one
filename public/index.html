Frequently asked

How do I use a guide?

Search for an issue, choose a location or category tag, then open the guide for the authority, evidence checklist and escalation steps.

Can I report an emergency here?

No. For immediate danger or a crime in progress, call 112 first. These pages explain non-emergency reporting routes.

How are new guides added?

Developers add a structured article to data/articles.json, assign tags from data/tags.json, then publish the update.
